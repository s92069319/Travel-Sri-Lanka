Explore Sri Lanka

Sinharaja forest

A UNESCO World Heritage rainforest reserve in Sri Lanka, rich in biodiversity, rare endemic species, dense greenery, and guided trekking trails through untouched tropical nature.

Sigiriya

An ancient rock fortress and UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring frescoes, water gardens, and panoramic views from the summit of King Kashyapa’s historic palace.

Adams Peak

A sacred pilgrimage mountain known for the “Sacred Footprint,” breathtaking sunrise views, and a spiritual hiking journey climbed by thousands each year.
